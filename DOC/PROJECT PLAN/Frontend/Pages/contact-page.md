# Contact Page

## Page Goal
Maximize qualified lead submissions with low friction and immediate alternate channels.

## User Intent
Reach the team now through preferred communication method.

## Primary CTA
- Submit Quote Request

## Secondary CTA
- Call Now / WhatsApp Now

## Section Order
1. Contact hero with response-time promise.
2. Multi-channel action bar (call, WhatsApp, form, bot).
3. Lead form with progressive fields.
4. Service area and hours block.
5. Trust and guarantee panel.
6. Map/location block.
7. FAQ mini-section.

## Form Content Model
- Required: name, phone, service needed, city, message.
- Optional: email, preferred callback window, urgency level.

## Form State Matrix
- Idle.
- Field validation error.
- Submitting.
- Success with lead reference.
- Recoverable submit error.
- Hard failure with fallback call and WhatsApp actions.

## SEO
- LocalBusiness and ContactPoint schema.

## Mobile Rules
- One-column form.
- Sticky submit and call bar near bottom while respecting nav safe area.
