# Blog Page

## Page Goal
Capture SEO traffic and funnel readers into service conversion.

## User Intent
Read practical electrical guidance and evaluate service provider expertise.

## Primary CTA
- Request Expert Help

## Secondary CTA
- Browse Related Services

## Section Order
1. Editorial hero and search bar.
2. Category/topic filters.
3. Featured article.
4. Article card grid with pagination.
5. Newsletter and quote CTA strip.

## Dynamic States
- Loading: article card skeleton.
- Empty: no-post guidance with service links.
- Error: retry + fallback links.

## SEO
- Article index metadata, pagination rel links, topical internal linking.

## Mobile Rules
- Search and filters stack vertically.
