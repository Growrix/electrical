# Home Page

## Page Goal
Convert first-time visitors into qualified leads while establishing trust quickly.

## User Intent
Understand what services are offered and contact immediately.

## Primary CTA
- Request Free Quote

## Secondary CTA
- Call 24/7 Service Line

## Section Order
1. Hero with location-aware headline and dual CTA.
2. Emergency contact strip.
3. Core services grid.
4. Why choose us trust section.
5. Testimonials highlight carousel.
6. Recent case studies spotlight.
7. Process timeline (book -> inspect -> fix -> verify).
8. FAQ preview section.
9. Lead capture short form.
10. Footer trust and legal links.

## Content Blocks
- Hero headline format: "Reliable Electrical Experts for {City}".
- Subhead: response time, certification, and guarantee.
- Trust metrics: jobs completed, average rating, response SLA.
- CTA copy: quote now, call now, WhatsApp now.

## Dynamic States
- Loading: skeleton for services/testimonials/case cards.
- Empty: hide empty blocks and replace with proof banners.
- Error: show retry CTA and fallback contact actions.

## SEO
- Metadata target keyword: local electrical services.
- Structured data: LocalBusiness + FAQ snippet.

## Mobile Rules
- Hero keeps one primary CTA above fold.
- Bottom nav + floating actions remain visible.
