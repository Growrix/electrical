# Blog Detail Page

## Page Goal
Provide high-value content and convert informational readers into leads.

## User Intent
Get accurate advice and decide if professional help is needed.

## Primary CTA
- Get Professional Assistance

## Secondary CTA
- Call for Immediate Help

## Section Order
1. Article hero with title, date, read time.
2. Table of contents.
3. Main content body with rich blocks.
4. Safety callout panels.
5. Related services CTA card.
6. Related posts.
7. End-of-article quote CTA.

## Dynamic States
- Loading: content skeleton.
- Error: route-level fallback to blog index.

## SEO
- Article schema, Open Graph, canonical URL.

## Mobile Rules
- TOC converts to collapsible drawer.
