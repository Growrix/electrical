# FAQ Page

## Page Goal
Reduce pre-sales friction by answering common concerns and routing to conversion.

## User Intent
Get quick answers about pricing, process, timing, and safety.

## Primary CTA
- Ask a New Question

## Secondary CTA
- Request Quote

## Section Order
1. FAQ hero and search input.
2. Category tabs.
3. Accordion list by category.
4. Contact fallback section.
5. CTA strip.

## Dynamic States
- Loading: accordion skeleton.
- Empty: no results with direct contact options.

## SEO
- FAQPage structured data.

## Mobile Rules
- Sticky search at top of viewport.
