# Service Detail Page

## Page Goal
Drive high-intent users to conversion by proving capability for a specific service.

## User Intent
Validate expertise, scope, timeline, and trust before contacting.

## Primary CTA
- Book This Service

## Secondary CTA
- Ask on WhatsApp

## Section Order
1. Service-specific hero with trust badges.
2. Problem signs and when to call.
3. Service scope checklist.
4. Pricing guidance and what affects cost.
5. Step-by-step work process.
6. Before/after gallery.
7. Related testimonials.
8. Related case studies.
9. Service-specific FAQ.
10. Inline lead form prefilled with service context.

## Content Blocks
- Include response SLA, availability, and guarantee policy.
- Include service-area list snippets.

## Dynamic States
- Loading: skeleton all sections.
- Empty: hide missing media or testimonials gracefully.
- Error: route-level fallback with route to contact page.

## SEO
- Service schema fields and keyword-aligned headings.

## Mobile Rules
- Sticky footer CTA bar with call and quote actions.
