# Case Study Detail Page

## Page Goal
Demonstrate process rigor and measurable outcomes to increase trust and conversions.

## User Intent
Understand how the team solves specific electrical challenges.

## Primary CTA
- Get a Similar Quote

## Secondary CTA
- Speak With Specialist

## Section Order
1. Project hero (title, location, category).
2. Problem context.
3. Solution breakdown.
4. Execution timeline.
5. Materials and standards used.
6. Outcome metrics and testimonial excerpt.
7. Related services.
8. CTA panel.

## Dynamic States
- Loading: article skeleton and metric cards.
- Error: fallback to case studies index.

## SEO
- Article schema + breadcrumb schema.

## Mobile Rules
- Timeline compresses to vertical stepper.
