# About Page

## Page Goal
Build credibility through company story, credentials, and team profile.

## User Intent
Validate legitimacy and professionalism before contacting.

## Primary CTA
- Contact Our Team

## Secondary CTA
- View Services

## Section Order
1. About hero and mission statement.
2. Company timeline.
3. Certifications and compliance badges.
4. Team profiles.
5. Service areas map.
6. Testimonial snippets.
7. Conversion CTA band.

## Dynamic States
- Loading: team/profile skeleton.
- Empty: fallback trust metrics.

## SEO
- Organization schema and service-area mentions.

## Mobile Rules
- Team cards as swipeable carousel.
