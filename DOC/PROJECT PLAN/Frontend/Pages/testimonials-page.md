# Testimonials Page

## Page Goal
Build trust with social proof and route visitors to a conversion action.

## User Intent
Verify service quality through real customer outcomes.

## Primary CTA
- Start Your Request

## Secondary CTA
- View Related Services

## Section Order
1. Trust hero with aggregate rating summary.
2. Filter by service category and location.
3. Testimonial card list.
4. Video or highlighted quotes block.
5. Conversion strip with quote CTA.

## Content Blocks
- Card fields: customer first name, location, service used, rating, quote, date.

## Dynamic States
- Loading: testimonial card skeleton.
- Empty: show trust alternatives (certifications/case study links).
- Error: show retry and quick contact options.

## SEO
- AggregateRating schema and internal linking to services.

## Mobile Rules
- Filters become dropdown.
- Cards keep readable hierarchy and tap-sized controls.
